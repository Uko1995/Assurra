02. Escrow Agreement

Assurra acts strictly as a neutral escrow agent, holding funds securely until predefined conditions of a transaction are met. We do not take ownership of the goods or funds in transit.

  • Funds are released only upon verifiable confirmation from both buyer and seller.
  • Timeline strictures must be adhered to as per the individual transaction contract.

warning Important Notice

Failure to respond to release requests within 72 hours may result in automatic disbursement of funds to the opposing party, barring an active dispute.

03. Platform Fees

We maintain transparent pricing with a standard 1.5% fee structure. Fees are calculated as a percentage of the total transaction volume and are deducted prior to final disbursement unless otherwise agreed by both parties.

04. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a disagreement, funds will be frozen in the escrow account. Both parties must submit compelling evidence to our resolution center. We operate with a strict 48-hour resolution target once all evidence is received.

05. KYC/AML Requirements

To ensure a secure environment and comply with international financial regulations, all users are subject to Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) checks. Transactions may be delayed or cancelled if required verification documents are not provided.
