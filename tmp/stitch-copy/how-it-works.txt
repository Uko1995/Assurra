Discover the four simple steps to flawless, risk-free transactions with Assurra Escrow. We bridge the gap between intent and fulfillment.
Create Escrow
Initiate the transaction via our dashboard or API. Every escrow is assigned a unique reference ID (e.g.,
TXN-2024-82930411
). API users can utilize idempotency keys to prevent duplicate transactions.
Status: INITIATED
Payment
The customer receives a secure Interswitch payment link. Funds are held in our CBN-regulated account until the transaction is complete.
Status: FUNDED
Notification
Once payment is verified, the merchant is instantly notified via email, SMS, and webhook to begin fulfillment.
Status: MERCHANT_NOTIFIED
Shipment
The merchant ships the item and uploads the tracking number to the Assurra portal, providing transparency for the buyer.
Status: SHIPPED
Delivery &amp; Inspection
Merchant marks the order as delivered. This triggers a 72-hour inspection window for the customer to verify the product quality.
Status: DELIVERED
Confirmation
The customer confirms receipt manually, or the system automatically releases funds after the 72-hour window expires.
Status: CONFIRMED
Status: AUTO_RELEASED
Payout
Funds are released to the merchant's verified Nigerian bank account in under 24 hours. Transaction complete.
Status: RELEASED

Cancellation
Unfunded escrows are automatically cancelled after 24 hours with no fees incurred by either party.

Dispute Window
Customers can raise a dispute within the 72h inspection window. We target a 48h resolution with evidence-based mediation.

Transparent Fees
A flat 1.5% fee applies. Customers pay the product price + fee; merchants receive the net amount instantly upon release.
